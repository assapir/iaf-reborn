# F-35I model credits

This work is based on "Lockheed Martin F35 Lightning II"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/lockheed-martin-f35-lightning-ii-db911de503c74285a8d506951df786d6) by andertan
(https://sketchfab.com/andertan) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: rescaled to 15.67 m, re-oriented, moving parts cut out and renamed, the nozzle interior decimated, a
tinted canopy glass and a pilot added (`tools/f35i/rig.py`).
