# F-35I model credits

- Model: based on "Lockheed Martin F35 Lightning II"
  (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/lockheed-martin-f35-lightning-ii-db911de503c74285a8d506951df786d6) by andertan
  (https://sketchfab.com/andertan), licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Changes: rescaled to 15.67 m, re-oriented, moving parts cut out and renamed, the nozzle interior decimated, a
  pilot added, and every texture replaced with our own (the download's textures were photographs).
- 116 Squadron badge (fin, in low-visibility greys): "IAF Squadron 116 Adir.png" by the IDF Spokesperson's Unit
  (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:IAF_Squadron_116_Adir.png), licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/); the low-visibility version (`textures/sqn116_lowvis.png`)
  is under the same licence.
- Skin, roundels and numbers: our own (`tools/f35i/rig.py`).
