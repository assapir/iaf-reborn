# F-16D Barak: pilot guide

## 1. This jet in the sim

The F-16D Barak is the Israeli Air Force's two-seat F-16D Block 40. In the sim it flies with 101 Squadron, "First Fighter", from Ramat David[^he101]. Its tail number is 547.

| item | the Barak |
|---|---|
| crew | two seats; the sim flies the front seat |
| engine | F110-GE-100 |
| gun | M61A1 20 mm, 511 rounds[^gdots] |
| decoys | 60 chaff, 60 flares |

**The cockpit.** The sim has one cockpit: the F-16's 3D cockpit. Its HUD is the Block 50 HUD with the real F-16 symbology. Both MFDs show the real formats in green. The RWR scope, the EHSI, the DED and the fault list are on their own small screens.

**What the sim models:**
- its own 6-DoF flight model, from published F-16 data;
- the cold start, switch by switch, with its waits and its faults;
- the tower on the radio, with clearances a training mission checks;
- clickable switches, knobs and handles in the 3D cockpit;
- the stores you hang on the Arming page: they drop and fire in flight;
- the gun's rounds, chaff and flares, and the master arm.

## 2. Before the flight: the Hangar

The **Hangar** is the first screen. Pick the Barak in the list of jets. Its card shows the squadron, the base, the tail number and your training progress. **Fly** opens Pick a mission.

The mission's briefing has three pages:
- **Briefing:** the base, the runway and its heading, the time, the wind, the time allowed, the objectives and the steps (How).
- **Map:** the base, the taxi route as a dashed line, the runway and the route. The mouse wheel zooms; a drag scrolls.
- **Arming:** the jet's front view with a slot per station. Drag a store from the Available list onto a slot. The mirror station gets the same store; hold `Shift` for one station only. The fuel and the take-off weight follow each change.

**Fly** starts the mission from any page.

**Settings → Devices** holds the rows for flying: Flight controls, Rudder, Throttle, Stick response and the Afterburner detent (set it with your throttle lever in its click). The **Keys** row opens the key list: click a command, then press its new key. The **Joystick** row gives each axis its role and each button its command.

The sim has no realism options: the 3D cockpit, the real HUD, the real displays and the flight model are always on.

## 3. The cold start-up

The Barak starts parked cold: engine off, no power, every screen dark, the canopy open. In the Start-up mission the checklist notebook opens at its START-UP page. A blue ring marks the switch of the step to take now.

Each step is a key or a click on its switch in the 3D cockpit.

| step | switch, place | key |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Main power: MAIN | MAIN PWR, ELEC panel, left console | `Ctrl + B` |
| 2. JFS: START 2 | JFS, ENG & JET START panel, left console | `Ctrl + J` |
| 3. Throttle to idle at 20 % RPM | throttle grip, left console | `1` |
| 4. Avionics power: ON | MMC, ST STA, MFD, UFC, AVIONIC POWER panel, right console | `Ctrl + A` |
| 5. INS: STOR HDG | INS knob, AVIONIC POWER panel, right console | `Ctrl + N` |
| 6. Canopy: close and lock | canopy handle, left canopy sill, aft | `Ctrl + K` |

Step 3 is the throttle moved up from OFF: the `1` key, `0`, your throttle axis, or a click on the grip.

### The waits

- After the JFS: wait for 20 % RPM, about 15 s.
- After the throttle: light-off 5 s later, then idle. At 52 % RPM the main generator comes on and the JFS switch springs back to OFF.
- After the avionics: the displays and the HUD come up 3 s later.
- After the INS: the alignment takes 90 s[^ins].

Idle and the displays come about 35 s after main power. The Start-up mission passes when the engine idles and the avionics are up within 5 minutes.

### The faults

A step out of order ends a training mission as a Fail:
- the JFS before main power: nothing runs;
- the throttle up before 20 % RPM: a hot start, and the engine shuts down[^chk];
- the avionics before the generator: no power;
- the INS without main power.

Each switch also goes back off by its key or a click, and its step is undone.

![The Barak's cockpit on the ground: engine running, MFDs and HUD up](shots/f16d/cockpit.webp)

### The sensor switches

FCR (the radar) and RDR ALT (the radar altimeter) are on the SNSR PWR panel, right console. A cold jet has them OFF, and no key moves them: click each one ON before you need the radar or the HUD's radar altitude.

## 4. Radio, taxi and take-off

### The radio menu

`Ctrl + T` opens the radio menu. Pick a call by its number; any other key closes it. In a training mission the call the moment needs is marked "(next)". Your callsign is Alpha.

| # | call | the tower's answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Request start-up | "cleared to start" |
| 2 | Request taxi | "taxi to runway 27" |
| 3 | Request line-up | "line up runway 27, wind calm" |
| 4 | Request take-off | "cleared for take-off" |

In the air the menu holds one call: Contact tower (landing). The tower answers "proceed to runway …".

The calls go in order. A call made too early gets "negative, hold position" and clears nothing. Request taxi needs the engine running. Request line-up works at the holding point, near the runway. Request take-off works once lined up.

### Acting without clearance

In the Take-off mission each phase waits for its clearance. Acting first ends the mission as a Fail:
- the engine turning (5 % RPM) before "cleared to start";
- the jet moving more than 10 m before the taxi clearance;
- entering the runway before "line up" or "cleared for take-off";
- rolling faster than 30 kt before "cleared for take-off".

On another runway end, the tower warns you and names the assigned runway. A take-off roll there fails the mission.

The Start-up mission needs no tower call.

### Taxi

Taxi on the taxiways to runway 27; the Map page and the notebook's TAXI / T-O tab show the way. Steer with the rudder: `Numpad 0` and `Decimal`, or your rudder axis. `B` brakes. Stop at the holding point and request line-up.

A training mission fails with the jet's centre off the pavement, with any damage, or a crash.

### The take-off

1. Line up on the centreline and request take-off.
2. Military power `6`, or afterburner `7` or `8`.
3. Rotate and lift off.
4. Gear up: `G`.
5. Climb out on the runway heading, within 10°.

The mission passes when the jet is airborne with the gear up and on the runway heading, within 15 minutes of the start. "Mission complete" shows; fly on, or `Esc` → End the flight for the debrief.

The debrief shows the runway, the lift-off speed, the reference speed, the largest offset from the centreline and the tower calls. The reference speed is 70 kt plus 3 kt per 1,000 lb of gross weight[^bms]. No rule fails on it.

## 5. The HUD in flight

The HUD draws the real F-16 symbology in green. In NAV: the airspeed tape on the left, the altitude tape on the right, the heading at the bottom, the pitch ladder and the flight path marker. Gear down, the altitude scale expands and the heading moves up over the flight path marker.

Looking away from the HUD's glass, the symbols move to the helmet display (DASH).

![The Barak's HUD just after take-off](shots/f16d/hud.webp)

### Brightness

- **The SYM wheel**, upper left of the ICP under the HUD: the mouse wheel over it, or a drag, turns it.
- `Shift + H` steps the brightness in eighths and turns the wheel with it.
- **The DAY / AUTO BRT / NIGHT switch**, on the HUD panel at the front of the right console: a click throws it one position. DAY keeps the wheel's level at any hour, AUTO dims it at sunset, NIGHT keeps it dim.

The sim keeps the wheel's and the switch's positions for the next flight.

### Modes and navigation

- `M` cycles the master mode: NAV, air-to-air, air-to-ground. `N` goes back to NAV.
- `W` and `Shift + W` step to the next and the previous waypoint.
- `A` cycles the autopilot: level, NAV (to the waypoint), off.

The MMC switch powers the HUD symbology: with it off the HUD is dark.

- `Insert` drops chaff.
- `Delete` drops flares.
- `J` turns the ECM jammer on and off.

## 7. Weapons

Every flight starts with the master arm SAFE. The notebook's WEAPONS tab lists these steps.

### Arming

1. ST STA ON: one of the four AVIONIC POWER switches, with `Ctrl + A`.
2. MASTER ARM to ARM: `Shift + A`, MISC panel, left of the front panel. The band shows "Master arm".
3. Gear up: nothing releases with the gear handle down.

### Air-to-ground

1. Hang the bombs on the Arming page before Fly: the default load carries none.
2. `[` selects them: the HUD shows the CCIP pipper.
3. Hold `Space`: the bombs leave as a pair when the pipper reaches the aim point.
4. `Shift + C` jettisons the tanks, then the bombs.

### Air-to-air

1. `]` selects the missiles: the HUD shows the AIM-9M's seeker circle.
2. `Return` locks the next target.
3. `Space` fires.
4. `Tab`, held, fires the gun.

The default load is an AIM-9M on stations 1, 2, 3, 7, 8 and 9 and a 370 gal tank on 4 and 6.

### The stores page

The right MFD's SMS page lists each station's store and count, the selected weapon and its mode. It shows RDY only with the master arm on ARM and ST STA on.

### The gun

The M61A1 fires 6,000 rounds a minute[^m61]: the 511 rounds last about 5 seconds.

## 8. Landing

1. `Ctrl + T`: Contact tower (landing). Wait for "proceed to runway …".
2. Gear down: `G`. The handle is left of the front panel. Check three green.
3. `F`: flaps up or down.
4. Fly the final at 13° AOA. The AOA indexer is left of the HUD.
5. At touchdown: throttle to idle `1`, wheel brakes `B`.
6. Taxi to the parking spot, then throttle OFF: `Shift + 1`.

`B` is the wheel brakes on the ground and the speed brake in the air.

**The approach speed.** At 13° AOA the F-16's flight manual gives about 136–141 kt at 21,000–22,000 lb, plus 4 kt for each 1,000 lb more[^to-f16]. The sim's F-16 flies within 5 kt of these figures.

`Shift + 1` moves the throttle over the idle detent to OFF and cuts the fuel. In a training mission before its pass, OFF with the engine running fails the mission.

## 9. Emergencies

The notebook's EMERGENCY tab holds three pages.

### Hot start

1. THROTTLE: OFF, `Shift + 1`.
2. FTIT: monitor.
3. FTIT above 500 °C: motor the engine with the JFS until below 200 °C.

In the sim a hot start is the throttle moved up from OFF before 20 % RPM: the engine shuts down. Restart with the JFS `Ctrl + J` once the RPM is below 20 %.

### Engine fire on the ground

1. THROTTLE: OFF, `Shift + 1`.
2. JFS: OFF, `Ctrl + J`.
3. MAIN PWR: OFF, `Ctrl + B`.
4. Canopy open, `Ctrl + K`, and leave the jet.

`X` fires the engine fire extinguisher.

### Ejection

Pull the ejection handle between the knees: press `E` three times. The sortie ends, and a training mission fails.

## 10. The checklist notebook

`H` raises the notebook (בד״ח in Hebrew) from your right thigh and stows it again. The flight keeps running.

| tab | the page |
|---|---|
| START-UP | the start-up steps, the step to take now marked |
| TAXI / T-O | the calls, the taxi, the take-off, the runway |
| RADIO | the base's frequencies, the calls and answers |
| LANDING | the landing steps |
| WEAPONS | arming, air-to-ground, air-to-air |
| EMERGENCY | hot start, fire, ejection |
| DATA | the mission, the base, the wind, the runway |
| GUIDE | this guide |

Turn the pages with a click on a tab, the ‹ and › at the page's foot, the mouse wheel over the notebook, or `PgUp` and `PgDn`.

## 11. Views

The cockpit opens on a wide view: the whole front panel in sight.

- **Zoom:** `=` and `-`, `Numpad +` and `Numpad -`, or the mouse wheel. Zoomed in, the MFD pages are readable.
- **Mouse look:** hold the right mouse button and move the mouse. The head stays where you leave it. A double right-click turns it back ahead.
- **Snap views:** the numpad keys turn the head while held, `Numpad 5` ahead. The joystick's hat does the same.
- **The left mouse button** works the switches and knobs.
