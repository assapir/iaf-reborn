# F-35I Adir: pilot guide

## 1. This jet in the sim

The F-35I Adir is Israel's single-seat stealth fighter. In the sim it flies with 116 Squadron, "Lions of the South", from Nevatim[^he116]. Its tail number is 937.

| item | the Adir |
|---|---|
| crew | one seat |
| engine | F135-PW-100, 28,000 lbf, 43,000 lbf with afterburner[^f135] |
| gun | GAU-22/A 25 mm, 180 rounds[^wf35] |
| decoys | 60 chaff, 60 flares |

**The cockpit.** The sim has one cockpit: the Adir's 3D cockpit. It has no HUD: the helmet display draws the flight symbols over the whole view. The panoramic display shows four portals, and the standby display sits under it.

**What the sim models:**
- its own 6-DoF flight model;
- the cold start, switch by switch, with its waits and its faults;
- the tower on the radio, with clearances a training mission checks;
- clickable switches, knobs and handles in the 3D cockpit;
- the stores you hang on the Arming page, in the bays and under the wings: they drop and fire in flight;
- the gun's rounds, chaff and flares, and the master arm.

## 2. Before the flight: the Hangar

The **Hangar** is the first screen. Pick the Adir in the list of jets. Its card shows the squadron, the base, the tail number and your training progress. **Fly** opens Pick a mission.

The mission's briefing has three pages:
- **Briefing:** the base, the runway and its heading, the time, the wind, the time allowed, the objectives and the steps (How).
- **Map:** the base, the taxi route as a dashed line, the runway and the route. The mouse wheel zooms; a drag scrolls.
- **Arming:** the jet's front view with a slot per station; the four bay stations are marked "internal". Drag a store from the Available list onto a slot. The mirror station gets the same store; hold `Shift` for one station only. The fuel and the take-off weight follow each change.

**Fly** starts the mission from any page.

**Settings → Devices** holds the rows for flying: Flight controls, Rudder, Throttle, Stick response and the Afterburner detent (set it with your throttle lever in its click). The **Keys** row opens the key list: click a command, then press its new key. The **Joystick** row gives each axis its role and each button its command.

The sim has no realism options: the 3D cockpit, the helmet display, the real displays and the flight model are always on.

## 3. The cold start-up

The Adir starts parked cold: engine off, no power, every display dark, the canopy open. In the Start-up mission the checklist notebook opens at its START-UP page. A blue ring marks the switch of the step to take now.

The real start is three selections: battery, IPP, engine[^ayton]. Each step is a key or a click on its switch in the 3D cockpit.

| step | switch, place | key |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Battery: ON | BATT, start panel, left console, forward | `Ctrl + B` |
| 2. IPP: START | IPP, start panel, left console | `Ctrl + J` |
| 3. Throttle to idle: the engine starts | throttle, left console; or ENG or START, start panel | `1` |
| 4. Canopy: close and lock | canopy lever, left wall, aft of the throttle | `Ctrl + K` |

Step 3 is the throttle moved up from OFF: the `1` key, `0`, your throttle axis, or a click on the throttle. A click on the ENG switch or the START button takes the same step.

### The waits

- After the IPP: wait 10 s for it to run up.
- After the throttle: the engine cranks, lights and runs up to idle by itself.
- The displays come up about 13 s after the battery, idle about 45 s after it.
- The avionics report ready 90 s after the battery[^ayton].

The Start-up mission passes when the engine idles and the avionics are up within 5 minutes.

### The faults

A step out of order ends a training mission as a Fail:
- the IPP before the battery: nothing runs;
- the throttle up before the IPP, or before the IPP has run up.

Each switch also goes back off by its key or a click, and its step is undone.

## 4. Radio, taxi and take-off

### The radio menu

`Ctrl + T` opens the radio menu. Pick a call by its number; any other key closes it. In a training mission the call the moment needs is marked "(next)". Your callsign is Alpha.

| # | call | the tower's answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Request start-up | "cleared to start" |
| 2 | Request taxi | "taxi to runway 26 left" |
| 3 | Request line-up | "line up runway 26 left, wind calm" |
| 4 | Request take-off | "cleared for take-off" |

In the air the menu holds one call: Contact tower (landing). The tower answers "proceed to runway …".

The calls go in order. A call made too early gets "negative, hold position" and clears nothing. Request taxi needs the engine running. Request line-up works at the holding point, near the runway. Request take-off works once lined up.

### Acting without clearance

In the Take-off mission each phase waits for its clearance. Acting first ends the mission as a Fail:
- the engine turning (5 % RPM) before "cleared to start";
- the jet moving more than 10 m before the taxi clearance;
- entering the runway before "line up" or "cleared for take-off";
- rolling faster than 30 kt before "cleared for take-off".

On another runway end, the tower warns you and names the assigned runway. A take-off roll there fails the mission.

The Start-up mission needs no tower call.

### Taxi

Taxi on the taxiways to runway 26L; the Map page and the notebook's TAXI / T-O tab show the way. Steer with the rudder: `Numpad 0` and `Decimal`, or your rudder axis. `B` brakes. Stop at the holding point and request line-up.

A training mission fails with the jet's centre off the pavement, with any damage, or a crash.

### The take-off

1. Line up on the centreline and request take-off.
2. Military power `6`, or afterburner `7` or `8`.
3. Rotate and lift off.
4. Gear up: `G`.
5. Climb out on the runway heading, within 10°.

The mission passes when the jet is airborne with the gear up and on the runway heading, within 15 minutes of the start. "Mission complete" shows; fly on, or `Esc` → End the flight for the debrief.

The debrief shows the runway, the lift-off speed, the reference speed, the largest offset from the centreline and the tower calls. The reference speed is about 165 kt[^wn]. No rule fails on it.

## 5. The helmet display in flight

The helmet display draws the flight symbols in green wherever you look. Looking ahead it shows a virtual HUD:
- the heading tape at the top;
- the airspeed boxed on the left, with the ground speed, Mach, g and AOA under it;
- the altitude boxed on the right, with the vertical speed and the radar altitude under it;
- the pitch ladder, the horizon and the flight path marker; the bank scale at the bottom;
- the master mode and the weapon at the lower left, the steerpoint at the lower right.

![The Adir's helmet display just after take-off](shots/f35i/hud.webp)

### Brightness

- **The HMD BRT knob**, on its pedestal: the mouse wheel over it, or a drag, turns it.
- `Shift + H` steps the brightness in eighths and turns the knob with it.
- **The MFD BRT knob** beside it sets the displays' brightness.

The helmet dims by itself after sunset. The sim keeps the knobs' positions for the next flight.

### Modes and navigation

- `M` cycles the master mode: NAV, air-to-air, air-to-ground. `N` goes back to NAV.
- `W` and `Shift + W` step to the next and the previous waypoint.
- `A` cycles the autopilot: level, NAV (to the waypoint), off.

## 7. Weapons

Every flight starts with the master arm SAFE. The notebook's WEAPONS tab lists these steps.

### Arming

1. MASTER ARM to ARM: `Shift + A`, left of the panoramic display. The band shows "Master arm".
2. Gear up: nothing releases with the gear handle down.

### Air-to-ground

1. Hang the bombs on the Arming page before Fly: the default load carries none.
2. `[` selects them: the helmet display shows the CCIP cue.
3. Hold `Space`: the bay doors open, the bomb leaves, the doors close.
4. `Shift + C` jettisons the bombs.

The bays take a GBU-31 or four GBU-39 on stations 4 and 8.

### Air-to-air

1. `]` selects the missiles: the AIM-120 in the bays.
2. `Return` locks the next target.
3. `Space` fires: the missile waits for the bay doors.
4. `Tab`, held, fires the gun.

The default load is an AIM-120 on the bay stations 5 and 7. The AIM-9X goes on the wing tips, stations 1 and 11.

### The stores page

The stores portal lists each station's store and count, the selected weapon and its mode. It shows ARM or SAFE over the weapon.

### The gun

The GAU-22/A fires 3,300 rounds a minute: the 180 rounds last about 3 seconds.

## 8. Landing

1. `Ctrl + T`: Contact tower (landing). Wait for "proceed to runway …".
2. Gear down: `G`. The handle is left of the panoramic display.
3. `F`: flaps up or down.
4. Fly the final at the on-speed AOA on the helmet display.
5. At touchdown: throttle to idle `1`, wheel brakes `B`.
6. Taxi to the parking spot, then throttle OFF: `Shift + 1`.

`B` is the wheel brakes on the ground and the speed brake in the air.

`Shift + 1` moves the throttle over the idle detent to OFF and cuts the fuel. In a training mission before its pass, OFF with the engine running fails the mission.

## 9. Emergencies

The notebook's EMERGENCY tab holds two pages.

### Engine fire on the ground

1. THROTTLE: OFF, `Shift + 1`.
2. BATT: OFF, `Ctrl + B`.
3. Canopy open, `Ctrl + K`, and leave the jet.

`X` fires the engine fire extinguisher.

### Ejection

Pull the ejection handle between the knees: press `E` three times. The sortie ends, and a training mission fails.

## 10. The checklist notebook

`H` raises the notebook (בד״ח in Hebrew) from your right thigh and stows it again. The flight keeps running.

| tab | the page |
|---|---|
| START-UP | the start-up steps, the step to take now marked |
| TAXI / T-O | the calls, the taxi, the take-off, the runway |
| RADIO | the base's frequencies, the calls and answers |
| LANDING | the landing steps |
| WEAPONS | arming, air-to-ground, air-to-air |
| EMERGENCY | fire, ejection |
| DATA | the mission, the base, the wind, the runway |
| GUIDE | this guide |

Turn the pages with a click on a tab, the ‹ and › at the page's foot, the mouse wheel over the notebook, or `PgUp` and `PgDn`.

## 11. Views

The cockpit opens on a wide view: the whole panoramic display in sight.

- **Zoom:** `=` and `-`, `Numpad +` and `Numpad -`, or the mouse wheel. Zoomed in, the portals are readable.
- **Mouse look:** hold the right mouse button and move the mouse. The head stays where you leave it. A double right-click turns it back ahead.
- **Snap views:** the numpad keys turn the head while held, `Numpad 5` ahead. The joystick's hat does the same.
- **The left mouse button** works the switches and knobs.
