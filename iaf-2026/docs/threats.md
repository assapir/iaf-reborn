# The 2026 threat set

**Terms:** AAA = anti-aircraft artillery; ARH = active radar homing; BVR = beyond visual range; CLOS = command to
line of sight; IR = infrared; MANPADS = man-portable air-defence system; SAM = surface-to-air missile; SARH =
semi-active radar homing; TEL / TELAR = launcher vehicle (with its own radar); "inferred" = our own reasoning;
"open" = no source found.

Research of 2 November 2026 for Modern IAF (plan.md, item D1). It picks the opposing units the first combat missions
need: SAMs, AAA, fighters, air-to-air missiles and strike targets, with the figures the unit / weapon database
(item D2) takes, and an open-licence model source for each. Every figure is from a public source; numbers that
Iran or its allies publish about their own weapons are marked as claims.

Licence rule for models and data: public domain, CC0, CC BY, GPL-2.0-or-later or a compatible licence; CC BY-SA,
ND and anything taken from a game are out.

## 1. Theatres and opponents

The year so far: the US and Israel struck Iran from 28 February 2026[^w-2026war]; the IDF said it had hit over 200
Iranian air-defence systems by 2 March[^ctp-0302]. A two-week ceasefire covering all fronts, Lebanon included, led to
talks in Islamabad on 11–12 April that ended without agreement[^w-islamabad]. Hezbollah joined the war on 2 March
(the 2026 Lebanon war)[^w-leb2026]. Status since late April: open (no later summary found).

The four home bases face two directions (inferred): Ramat David (north) flies against Lebanon and Syria; Nevatim,
Ramon and Hatzerim (Negev) fly the long-range missions to Iran and Yemen.

**First set for the sim:**
1. **S-300PMU-2:** the long-range threat on the Negev bases' Iran missions; the best public figures of any long-range
   system (range, altitude, radar bands, launch interval).
2. **3rd Khordad:** Iran's own medium-range system, in combat in 2025 and 2026; radar bands and fire-unit capacity
   published; missile speed open (inferred: use a Ta'er-2B speed of the Buk class, Mach 4, until a figure is found).
3. **Tor-M1:** Iran's point defence around the targets; full figures.
4. **2K12 Kub / Fater-1:** the radar SAM on Yemen missions and the Syrian remnant; full figures except the g limit.
5. **Misagh-2:** the MANPADS every opponent holds in some form; full figures.
6. **ZSU-23-4:** the AAA that low-level attacks meet; full figures.

Deferred: Bavar-373 and 15th Khordad (only Iranian claims), the 358 (no performance figures), Pantsir-S1 (good data,
but Syria's were destroyed and Iran is not an operator), Buk / Ra'ad (Iran's Ra'ad has no own figures).

## 4. Ground and sea targets

- Ballistic-missile launchers and their shelters (Iran; about 300 launchers struck by 3 March 2026)[^ctp-0303].
- Air-defence sites and radars (§2)[^ctp-0302].
- Parked aircraft and hardened shelters at airbases (Isfahan, Tabriz, Hamadan)[^aero-iriaf].
- Rocket and drone launchers in south Lebanon[^olj-launchers].
- Tunnel shafts and underground sites (Gaza)[^newarab-tunnels].
- Ports, fuel and power plants (Hodeidah, Sanaa)[^nbc-hodeidah][^almonitor-sanaa].
- Drone launch and storage sites (Iraq, Lebanon, Yemen)[^wi-kh].
- Fast-attack and mine-laying boats (Strait of Hormuz)[^toi-boats].
