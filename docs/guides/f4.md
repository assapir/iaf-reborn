# F-4E pilot guide (realistic settings)

This guide is for flying the F-4E Kurnass (the two-seat Phantom II: the game's model has two crew under a tandem
canopy, and its Reference text reads "McDonnell Douglas Phantom II … two-seat all-weather fighter") in this game with
every realistic option switched on. The numbers come from the game's own data and tests. Where the game differs from
the real jet, the guide says so.

![The F-4E cockpit on the runway](shots/f4/cockpit_runway.png)

---

## 1. Settings

Set these on the Preferences screen before the flight. The original 1998 game is the default everywhere, so each of
these has to be switched on.

### Extras tab

| Row                    | Set to                            | What it does                                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Flight data            | Real aircraft                     | The F-4E (2 × J79-GE-17) numbers: weight, fuel, thrust, roll, g limits, fuel flow, ceiling, drag chute |
| Weapon data            | Real weapons                      | Real missile weights, speeds and ranges, the APQ-120 radar range, 639 gun rounds at 6,000 rounds/min |
| HUD                    | Real                              | The F-4E's AN/ASG-26 lead-computing optical sight (red reticle)                                |
| HUD pitch ladder       | Conformal                         | The ladder lies on the real horizon (only matters with the original HUD)                            |
| Sky                    | Atmospheric                       | A physical sky with the sun, and haze that takes its colour                                         |
| Terrain close up       | Detailed                          | Ground detail below about 500 m                                                                     |
| Imagery Israel         | Survey 2 m (or Survey 2 m modern) | 2 m photos of Israel, if setup converted them                                                       |

## 2. The jet in numbers

With Flight data = Real the F-4E row comes from public figures (Jane's, T.O. 1F-4E-1 limits, GE engine data), checked
by the game's validation test. The original 1998 F-4 is badly underpowered (subsonic at sea level) and rolls at half
the real rate; the Real set fixes that.

| Item                                   | In the game             | Real F-4E                                      |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Empty weight                           | 30,330 lb               | about 30,330 lb (Jane's)                       |
| Internal fuel                          | 12,060 lb               | 1,855 gal = 12,060 lb (block 41+)              |
| Thrust, military / full afterburner    | 23,740 / 35,800 lbf     | 2 × 11,870 / 2 × 17,900 lbf (J79-GE-17)        |
| 1 g stall speed                        | 145 kt (the original's) | about 150–165 kt at 40,000 lb (uncertain)      |
| g limits                               | +7.33 / −3              | +7.33 at 37,500 lb or less / −3 (T.O. 1F-4E-1) |
| Max roll rate                          | 150°/s (uncertain)      | about 120–180°/s at 350–450 kt (uncertain)     |
| Max speed, sea level                   | 780 kt (Mach 1.18)      | about 750 KIAS placard (Mach 1.13–1.19)        |
| Max speed, 40,000 ft                   | 1,239 kt (Mach 2.16)    | Mach 2.2–2.23                                  |
| Climb at 350 kt, sea level             | about 28,300 ft/min     | 41,300 ft/min                                  |
| Fuel flow, military / full afterburner | 20,000 / 71,000 lb/h    | about 20,000 / about 71,000 lb/h               |
| Service ceiling                        | 58,750 ft               | 58,750 ft at maximum power (100 ft/min)        |

What that means in the cockpit:

- **Fuel.** At full afterburner the internal fuel lasts about 10 minutes, at military power about 36 minutes. The
  3,700 lb tanks ("3700LB") go on stations 1, 5 and 9.
- **Drag chute (Shift+B)**: with Flight data = Real the 16 ft ring-slot chute adds real drag (deploy below 200 KIAS, the
  T.O. limit; there is no failure model).
- **Nose-wheel steering** comes from the rudder pedals, ±70° (below about 70 kt on the real jet), wheelbase 23.3 ft.
- **Two engines**: two sets of RPM, temperature and throttle needles on the panel.

## 3. Start-up, take-off and landing

### On the runway

A mission that starts at an airbase puts you on the runway with the gear down, full flaps, the brakes on and the
throttle at idle. The engines are already running at the lineup point. If you start elsewhere on the base, the first
throttle key starts them, and the RPM then climbs 15 % per second.

### Take-off

1. Check the gear is down and the flaps are out (**F**).
2. Release the brakes (**B**).
3. Throttle to full afterburner: **8**. The afterburner lights only after the engines reach 100 %, about 2 s from
   military power.
4. Keep the runway centreline with the rudder (**Numpad 0** / **Decimal**, or the pedals).
5. At **145 kt**, pull gently back until the flight path is above the horizon.
6. Gear up (**G**) and flaps up (**F**). Keep the gear speed below 300 kt.

### Landing

1. **Downwind**: 2,000 ft above the ground, **200 kt**. Gear down (**G**), flaps out (**F**).
2. **Base**: throttle 60–80 % (keys **2** to **4**), about 20° of bank, slow to **165 kt**, aim for a 4° approach.
3. **Final**: line up with the runway on a 4° glide slope (the gear-down HUD's ILS lines are not drawn with the Real sight: use the runway and the panel's gauges).
4. Over the threshold: throttle to idle (**1**) and flare gently.
5. After touchdown: brakes (**B**); then the drag chute (**Shift+B**) below 200 kt.

With Real landing limits and Easy landing off, a touchdown faster than **4 m/s (about 790 ft/min)** or more than
**15° nose-up** is a crash. These speeds come from the game's own training lessons, which are the same for every jet.

![Final approach, gear down](shots/f4/landing_final.png)

### Tower and autopilot

- **Ctrl+T** calls the tower near an Israeli base.
- **A** cycles the autopilot: off → level → NAV (follows the waypoints) → off. **W** / **Shift+W** pick the next /
  previous waypoint.

## 4. Handling

- **The stick on the keyboard** is all or nothing: a tap gives full deflection at once, as in the original. A joystick
  is much smoother.
- **No fly-by-wire limiter.** Nothing stops you from pulling into a stall: at low speed a hard pull stalls the wings,
  and with Realistic spins on you can spin. Recover with the stick forward and the rudder against the spin, then pull
  out of the dive.
- **Flight-path hold.** With the stick centred the jet holds its flight path.
- **g-effects.** With No blackouts off, a hard pull greys and blacks out the screen.

## 5. The Real sight (AN/ASG-26 LCOSS)

The F-4E's sight is the AN/ASG-26 lead-computing optical sight: the Real switch draws it in **red** (Heatblur F-4E
manual, from T.O. 1F-4E-1/-34). **The sight shows the aiming reticle**: airspeed, altitude and heading are on the
panel's gauges, and missile status, IN RANGE and SHOOT are lights on the panel.

![The Real sight in NAV: only the LCOSS reticle](shots/f4/hud_nav.png)

- **Pipper**: a 2 mr dot; an inner ring of 25 mr (dashed) and an outer ring of 50 mr (solid) with three roll tabs.
- **Range bar** inside the outer ring, from 6 o'clock counter-clockwise, **with a radar lock only**: guns 1,000 ft at
  6 o'clock plus 1,000 ft per hour (up to 6,667 ft); missiles 3,000 ft per hour (up to 20,000 ft).
- **A-A**: gyro lead with the radar range (1,000 ft without a lock).
- **A-G**: the real sight has a manual depression and **no CCIP**; ours puts the reticle on the predicted impact
  (the original's CCIP pipper), a difference from the real jet.

## 6. Radar (AN/APQ-120)

With Weapon data = Real the radar detects at **61 km** (33 NM: average 30–35 NM). The keys are the same as in every jet:

| Key                           | Action                                              |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **R**                         | Radar on: A-A, then toggles A-A ↔ A-G               |
| **Q**                         | Mode: A-A LRS → TWS → ACM; A-G GMT ↔ MAP            |
| **S**                         | Standby                                             |
| **.** / **,**                 | Range scale up / down                               |
| **Return** / **Shift+Return** | Next / previous contact (in LRS this locks it: STT) |
| Click a blip                  | Lock that contact                                   |
| **Backspace**                 | Drop the lock                                       |
| **\\** (hold)                 | Boresight: a ±12° lock cone straight ahead          |

The radar does not see targets lower than 30 m above the ground or behind terrain. Locking a target tells it you
locked it (and its RWR sounds).

![The radar display with a locked contact](shots/f4/mfd_radar_stt.png)

## 7. Displays

The F-4E has **one display**, the radar scope, which starts on the **radar**. The **RWR** is a separate dial on the
panel. **T** brings up the TSD (tactical map) on the display, **D** the damage report, **V** / **PgUp** / **PgDn**
slide the panel. The F-4E's panel has no attitude ball: only the lens attitude indicator.

---

## 8. Self-protection

- **RWR**: the panel dial shows emitters and missiles around you; a new threat sounds a tone, a missile launch at you
  the launch warning.
- **Chaff (Insert)** and **flares (Delete)**: the F-4E carries **90 chaff and 60 flares**. Each press releases one.
  At release, every missile of the decoy's kind launched at you rolls once: chaff fools a radar missile with 10 %
  (30 % above 4 g), a flare an IR missile with 33 % (50 % above 4 g), and **never with the afterburner lit**.
- **ECM (J)**: only with the **ECM pod** loaded on station 4 or 5. Switching it on jams each radar-guided missile already
  flying at you with a 60 % chance. It has no effect on SAMs.

## 9. Weapons

**M** cycles the master mode NAV → A-A → A-G, and **N** returns to NAV. **]** selects the next air-to-air weapon and
**[** the next air-to-ground one. **Space** fires the selected weapon and **Tab** fires the gun. Nothing fires with the
gear handle down.

What the F-4E can load on the Arming screen (stations 1 and 9 are the outer wing pylons, 2 and 8 the inner ones, 3, 4,
6, 7 the fuselage, 5 the centreline):

| Store                   | Stations                   |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------- |
| AIM-9D                  | 1, 2, 8, 9, and ×2 on 4, 6 |
| Shafrir 2               | 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 9           |
| AIM-7B Sparrow          | 3, 4, 6, 7                 |
| AGM-45 Shrike           | 3, 4, 6, 7                 |
| ZUNNI rockets (×25)     | 1, 2, 8, 9                 |
| MK-82 (×6), CBU-87 (×6) | 1, 2, 5, 8, 9              |
| MK-83 (×3), MK-84       | 1, 2, 5, 8, 9              |
| Fuel tank ("3700LB")    | 1, 5, 9                    |
| ECM pod                 | 4, 5                       |

The F-4E has no FLIR, TV, laser or HARM weapons.

### Gun (M61A1)

With Real data: **639 rounds**, 6,000 rounds/min, 1,030 m/s: about 6 s of firing. The sight's gyro lead uses the radar
range when you have a lock; the range bar then shows it.

![The gun: the LCOSS reticle with the range bar](shots/f4/sight_gun.png)

### IR missiles: AIM-9D, Shafrir 2

| Missile   | Real data                                   |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| AIM-9D    | 88.5 kg, Mach 2.5, 18 km, rear-aspect, 12 g |
| Shafrir 2 | 93 kg, Mach 2.1, 5 km, rear-aspect          |

1. **]** until the IR missile: a growl, then a higher tone when the seeker **locks**.
2. **With a radar lock** the seeker is slaved to the locked target within its cone off the nose.
3. Beyond **half** its range the seeker sees only a jet **with its afterburner lit**.
4. Fire with **Space** when locked.

A **rear-aspect** seeker (Real data) gives no tone on a target coming at you or standing still: get behind it.

![AIM-9D: the reticle with the seeker locked](shots/f4/sight_srm.png)

### Radar missile: AIM-7B Sparrow

Real data: 176 kg, Mach 2.5, 7 km (both uncertain, early-variant figures), **semi-active**: needs your lock until it hits.

1. Radar on (**R**), lock a target.
2. **]** until the Sparrow.
3. Fire (**Space**) inside its range and **keep the lock**: it loses its guidance as soon as the lock drops or changes.

![AIM-7B with the radar lock](shots/f4/sight_mrm.png)

### Bombs and rockets

MK-82 / MK-83 / MK-84, the CBU-87 cluster and ZUNNI rockets. **[** to the store: ours puts the sight's reticle on the
predicted impact (the real F-4E sets a manual depression).

2. Put the pipper on the target and hold **Space**: the ripple releases one store per interval while you hold it.
3. **Delayed release**: if the impact is below the HUD's bottom edge, hold **Space** with the target under the pipper
   at the edge; the bombs go when the time-to-go reaches 0.9 s (positive g, less than 90° of bank).
4. The ripple quantity and spacing are set on the stores page (default: 2 bombs, 10 m apart).

The game's training warns against GP bombs below 2,000 ft above the ground. There is no fuse or arming time: a bomb
dropped from any height bursts at the ground.

![Bombing: the reticle on the predicted impact](shots/f4/sight_bomb.png)

### AGM-45 Shrike

Real data: 177 kg, Mach 2, 40 km. **[** to the Shrike: the HARM page comes up on the display and lists the radar sites
inside a ±15° cone ahead, out to 20 NM; **Ctrl+Return** steps to the next target. Fire with **Space** when the target is
near the sight's centre; after the launch the Shrike chases its target whether or not it keeps emitting.

![The Shrike: the HARM page](shots/f4/mfd_shrike.png)

### Jettison

**Shift+C**: the first press drops the fuel tanks, the second the bombs.
