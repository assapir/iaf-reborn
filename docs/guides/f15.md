# F-15 pilot guide (realistic settings)

This guide tells you how to fly the F-15C Baz in this game with every realistic option switched on. The F-15C is the
single-seat Eagle: the game's model, and its Reference text "F-15C/D Eagle, Israel Defence Force name: Baz". The numbers
come from the game's own data and tests. Where the game differs from the real jet, the guide says so. Section 11
explains the abbreviations.

![The F-15 cockpit on the runway](shots/f15/cockpit_runway.png)

each one on.

## 2. The jet in numbers

With Flight data = Real the F-15C row comes from the USAF Standard Aircraft Characteristics chart ("F-15C Eagle,
two F100-PW-220", Feb 1992), checked by the game's validation test.

| Item                                   | In the game                 | Real F-15C                             |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Empty weight                           | 28,476 lb                   | 28,476 lb                              |
| Internal fuel                          | 13,455 lb                   | 13,455 lb (JP-4)                       |
| Thrust, military / full afterburner    | 28,740 / 46,900 lbf         | 2 × 14,370 / 2 × 23,450 lbf            |
| 1 g stall speed                        | 130 kt (full internal fuel) | 135 kt at 45,713 lb, power off         |
| g limits                               | +9 / −3                     | +7.33 at 37,400 lb, up to +9 light; −3 |
| Max roll rate                          | 180°/s                      | about 180–220°/s                       |
| Max speed, sea level                   | 805 kt                      | about 800 KCAS                         |
| Max speed, 40,000 ft                   | 1,238 kt (Mach 2.16)        | 1,309 kt (Mach 2.28) at 35,000 ft      |
| Climb at 350 kt, sea level             | about 42,000 ft/min         | 55,960 ft/min at 41,286 lb             |
| Fuel flow, military / full afterburner | 21,000 / 98,000 lb/h        | about 21,000 / about 98,000 lb/h       |
| Service ceiling                        | 65,000 ft                   | 65,000 ft                              |

What that means in the cockpit:

- **Fuel.** At full afterburner the internal fuel lasts about 8 minutes, at military power about 38 minutes. The F-15
  carries 600-gallon tanks ("4000 LB") on stations 2, 5 and 8.
- **Speeds the game's own training uses** (the training lessons are the same for every jet): rotate at **145 kt**,
  downwind at **200 kt** and 2,000 ft above the ground, base and final at **165 kt**, gear down below **300 kt**. The
  F-15's stall is near **130 kt**, so keep the approach above about 150 kt.
- **Nose-wheel steering** comes from the rudder pedals, ±45° (the real jet's manoeuvring range), wheelbase 17.8 ft.

## 3. Start-up, take-off and landing

### On the runway

A mission that starts at an airbase puts you on the runway with the gear down, full flaps, the brakes on and the
throttle at idle. The engines are already running at the lineup point. If you start elsewhere on the base, the first
throttle key starts them, and the RPM then climbs 15 % per second.

### Take-off

1. Check the gear is down and the flaps are out (**F**).
2. Release the brakes (**B**).
3. Throttle to full afterburner: **8**. The afterburner lights only after the engines reach 100 %, about 2 s from
   military power.
4. Keep the runway centreline with the rudder (**Numpad 0** / **Decimal**, or the pedals).
5. At **145 kt**, pull gently back until the velocity vector is above the horizon.
6. Gear up (**G**) and flaps up (**F**). Keep the gear speed below 300 kt.

![Take-off roll, HUD gear down](shots/f15/takeoff_roll.png)

### Landing

1. **Downwind**: 2,000 ft above the ground, **200 kt**. Gear down (**G**), flaps out (**F**).
2. **Base**: throttle 60–80 % (keys **2** to **4**), about 20° of bank, slow to **165 kt**, aim for a 4° approach.
3. **Final**: put the velocity vector on the cross of the ILS lines on the HUD (the localizer and a 4° glide slope).
   With the gear down the HUD shows the **AoA scale** on the left: hold the approach angle of attack to keep your speed
   up.
4. Over the threshold: throttle to idle (**1**) and bring the velocity vector slowly up to the horizon.
5. After touchdown: brakes (**B**: the speed brake in the air, the wheel brakes on the ground).

With Real landing limits and Easy landing off, a touchdown faster than **4 m/s (about 790 ft/min)** or more than
**15° nose-up** is a crash.

![Final approach, the gear-down HUD with the AoA scale](shots/f15/landing_final.png)

### Tower and autopilot

- **Ctrl+T** calls the tower near an Israeli base.
- **A** cycles the autopilot: off → level → NAV (follows the waypoints) → off. **W** / **Shift+W** pick the next /
  previous waypoint.

## 4. Handling

- **The stick on the keyboard** is all or nothing: a tap gives full deflection at once, as in the original. A joystick
  is much smoother.
- **Mechanical flight controls.** The stick moves the surfaces directly, so you own the limits: at low speed a hard
  pull stalls the wings, and with Realistic spins on you can spin. Recover with the stick forward and the rudder against the
  spin, then pull out of the dive.
- **Flight-path hold.** With the stick centred the jet holds its flight path.
- **g-effects.** With No blackouts off, a hard pull greys and blacks out the screen.

## 5. The Real HUD

The Real HUD draws the F-15A/C HUD from the flight manual (TO 1F-15A-1): **moving tapes read at fixed carets**. **H**
changes the HUD colour.

![The Real HUD in NAV](shots/f15/hud_nav.png)

### Scales and windows

- **Airspeed** (left): a moving tape, **increasing downward**, with a fixed caret.
- **Altitude** (right): a barometric tape (above sea level), increasing upward. It is the HUD's **only altitude**.
- **Heading** (top): a 30° window with two-digit labels and a caret below it.
- **Pitch ladder**: 5° rungs, climb rungs solid, dive rungs dashed. Straight up the **zenith ⊖ "90"**, straight down
  the **nadir ⊗**.
- **Velocity vector**: a circle with wings and a fin; the aircraft symbol (`-W-`) above it.
- **G** under the airspeed tape; **Mach** under it in the A-A modes only.
- **NAV window** (lower right, three lines): the steerpoint number and `NAV`, the range (`N 4.7`), the minutes to go.
- **Steering**: a bank steering bar to the steerpoint.
- **Gun cross** `+`: only in a weapon mode.
- **Gear down**: the **AoA scale** on the left (0–45; ours shows degrees as units).

### Approach and shoot cues

The HUD guides the approach with the game's ILS lines (the real HUD uses a flight-director cross). The real jet's SHOOT
cue is a light on the canopy bow, outside the HUD.

### The DASH helmet (looking aside)

With the Real HUD on, looking aside (**Shift+arrows**, **Numpad** snap views or padlock **F3**, far enough that the
panel pans away) shows the F-15's Elbit **DASH** helmet display. The real DASH is a
20° circular, green display on the visor; the IAF fielded it with the Python 4 in 1993–94. The game draws the symbology
of its descendant, the JHMCS:

- the **aiming cross** in the middle: where your head points;
- the **line-of-sight circle** around it: its gap points where you are looking from the nose (a gap at 9 o'clock:
  looking left);
- left: **G** over the **airspeed**, then **ARM** / NAV and the selected missiles (e.g. 2 SRM);
- right: the **altitude**, then the steerpoint (005>01);
- above: the **heading your head is looking at**;
- a **box** on the radar target and the **seeker diamond** on what the AIM-9 / Python seeker tracks, when they are
  inside the 20° circle. Look at a target with an AIM-9 or Python selected and the seeker follows your head.

![F-15 DASH: looking left with the Real HUD on](shots/f15/dash.png)

## 6. Radar (AN/APG-63)

With Weapon data = Real the radar detects at **135 km**. The keys are the same as in every jet:

| Key                           | Action                                              |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **R**                         | Radar on: A-A, then toggles A-A ↔ A-G               |
| **Q**                         | Mode: A-A LRS → TWS → ACM; A-G GMT ↔ MAP            |
| **S**                         | Standby                                             |
| **.** / **,**                 | Range scale up / down                               |
| **Return** / **Shift+Return** | Next / previous contact (in LRS this locks it: STT) |
| Click a blip                  | Lock that contact                                   |
| **Backspace**                 | Drop the lock                                       |
| **\\** (hold)                 | Boresight: a ±12° lock cone straight ahead          |

The radar sees targets from 30 m above the ground, in line of sight. Locking a target tells it you locked it (and its
RWR sounds).

![The radar display with a locked contact](shots/f15/mfd_radar_stt.png)

## 7. MFDs

The F-15 has three displays: the left one starts on the **radar**, the right one on the **RWR**, the middle one on the
**TSD** (tactical map). **T** brings up the TSD, **D** the damage report, **V** / **PgUp** / **PgDn** slide the panel.

---

## 8. Self-protection

- **RWR**: the right display shows emitters and missiles around you; a new threat sounds a tone, a missile launch at
  you the launch warning.
- **Chaff (Insert)** and **flares (Delete)**: the F-15 carries **120 chaff and 75 flares**. Each press releases one. At
  release, every missile of the decoy's kind launched at you rolls once. Chaff fools a radar missile with 10 % (30 %
  above 4 g). A flare fools an IR missile with 33 % (50 % above 4 g), **only with the afterburner off**.
- **ECM (J)**: only with the **ECM pod** loaded on station 5. Switching it on jams each radar-guided missile already
  flying at you with a 60 % chance. SAMs keep guiding.

## 9. Weapons

**M** cycles the master mode NAV → A-A → A-G, and **N** returns to NAV. **]** selects the next air-to-air weapon and
**[** the next air-to-ground one. **Space** fires the selected weapon and **Tab** fires the gun. The weapons are live
with the gear handle up.

What the F-15 can load on the Arming screen (stations 1 and 9 are the wing pylons, 3, 4, 6, 7 the fuselage, 5 the
centreline):

| Store                                | Stations                   |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| AIM-9L / AIM-9M, Python 3 / Python 4 | 1, 9 (×2 each)             |
| AMRAAM                               | 1, 9 (×2 each), 3, 4, 6, 7 |
| AIM-7M / AIM-7F Sparrow              | 3, 4, 6, 7                 |
| MK-82                                | 2, 8, and ×6 on 5          |
| MK-83                                | 2, 8, and ×3 on 5          |
| MK-84                                | 2, 5, 8                    |
| Fuel tank, 600 gal ("4000 LB")       | 2, 5, 8                    |
| ECM pod                              | 5                          |

The F-15C carries air-to-air missiles, the MK-82, MK-83 and MK-84 bombs, and its gun.

### Gun (M61A1)

With Real data: **940 rounds**, 6,000 rounds/min, 1,030 m/s: about 9 s of firing.

- **A-A gun** (**]** to the gun): the **LCOS** gunsight: an outer ring with 12 ticks, a dashed inner circle, the centre
  pipper and a thick range arc that unwinds with the radar range.
- **A-G gun** (**[** to the gun): the same reticle on the predicted impact.

![The gun: the LCOS reticle with the range arc](shots/f15/hud_gun.png)

### IR missiles: AIM-9L / AIM-9M, Python 3, Python 4

| Missile         | Real data                                  |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| AIM-9L / AIM-9M | 86 kg, Mach 2.5, 35.4 km                   |
| Python 3        | 120 kg, Mach 3.5, 15 km, 30° off-boresight |
| Python 4        | 120 kg, Mach 3.5, 15 km, 60° off-boresight |

1. **]** until the IR missile. The HUD shows the missile circle and the seeker circle; a growl, then a higher tone
   when the seeker **locks**.
2. **With a radar lock** the seeker is slaved to the locked target within its cone off the nose.
3. **Off-boresight**: in the free-look view (**Shift+arrows**) or padlock (**F3**), the helmet sight lets the seeker
   take the target you look at, within the missile's cone.
4. Beyond **half** its range the seeker sees only a jet **with its afterburner lit**.
5. Fire with **Space** when locked.

![AIM-9L with the seeker locked](shots/f15/hud_srm.png)

### Radar missiles: AMRAAM, AIM-7M / AIM-7F

| Missile             | Real data                                                             |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AMRAAM (AIM-120A/B) | 157 kg, Mach 4, 70 km, **active**: guides itself after the launch     |
| AIM-7M / AIM-7F     | 230 kg, Mach 4, 70 km, **semi-active**: needs your lock until it hits |

1. Radar on (**R**), lock a target.
2. **]** until the radar missile. The HUD shows the **TD box** on the target, the **ASE circle** with its **steering
   dot**, and inboard of the altitude tape the **range scale**: the radar range at the top, the target's range caret
   with the closure (`490>`), the MAX and MIN ticks, and **IN RNG** when the target is inside them.
3. Steer the dot into the circle and fire (**Space**). Inside the minimum range a **breakaway X** fills the HUD.
4. A **Sparrow** loses its guidance as soon as the lock drops or changes. Keep the lock until it hits.

![AIM-7M: the ASE circle, steering dot and the range scale](shots/f15/hud_mrm.png)

### Bombs

MK-82, MK-83, MK-84. The F-15A/C bombs with the **bomb fall line**: the HUD draws it from the velocity vector to a
**target square** on the predicted impact.

1. **[** to the bombs.
2. Dive so the fall line runs through the target and the square sits on it; hold **Space**: the ripple releases one
   store per interval while you hold it.
3. The ripple quantity and spacing are set on the stores page.

![Bombing: the fall line from the velocity vector to the target square](shots/f15/hud_bomb.png)

### Jettison

**Shift+C**: the first press drops the fuel tanks, the second the bombs.

## 11. Abbreviations

| Term   | Meaning                                            |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| A-A    | air-to-air                                         |

| GMT    | ground moving target                               |
| HUD    | head-up display                                    |
| IAF    | Israeli Air Force                                  |
| ILS    | instrument landing system                          |
| IR     | infrared (heat-seeking)                            |
| JHMCS  | Joint Helmet-Mounted Cueing System                 |
| KCAS   | knots calibrated airspeed                          |
| kt     | knots                                              |
| lbf    | pound-force (thrust)                               |
| LCOS   | lead-computing optical sight                       |
| LRS    | long-range search                                  |
| MFD    | multi-function display                             |
| NAV    | navigation mode                                    |
| RPM    | engine speed, as % of maximum                      |
| RWR    | radar warning receiver                             |
| SAM    | surface-to-air missile                             |
| SRM    | short-range missile: the IR missiles               |
