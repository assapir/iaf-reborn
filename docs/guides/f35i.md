# F-35I pilot guide (realistic settings)

This guide is for flying our **F-35I Adir** in this game with every realistic option switched on. The F-35I is our
addition to the game (docs/f35i.md): an F-35A airframe with the F135-PW-100 in IAF markings (tail 937, 116 Squadron),
with a modelled cockpit around one panoramic display, and the helmet display as its HUD. The
numbers come from public sources (Lockheed Martin, USAF); where they are estimates the guide says so.

**To fly it**: Preferences → Extras → **F-35I replaces** → pick a Jet list button (the Lavi's, for example). That
button is relabelled F-35I (Hebrew אדיר) and flies the F-35I in every training and Jet list mission.

![The F-35I cockpit on the runway](shots/f35i/cockpit_runway.png)

## 1. Settings

Set these on the Preferences screen before the flight. The original 1998 game is the default everywhere, so each of
these has to be switched on.

### Extras tab

| Row                    | Set to                            | What it does                                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Flight data            | Real aircraft                     | The F-35I always flies its real F-35A numbers (F135-PW-100): this row does not change it |
| Weapon data            | Real weapons                      | Real missile weights, speeds and ranges, the APG-81 radar range (148 km, U), 180 gun rounds |
| HUD                    | Real                              | The helmet's green virtual HUD (Gen III)                                                            |
| HUD pitch ladder       | Conformal                         | The ladder lies on the real horizon (only matters with the original HUD)                            |
| Sky                    | Atmospheric                       | A physical sky with the sun, and haze that takes its colour                                         |
| Terrain close up       | Detailed                          | Ground detail below about 500 m                                                                     |
| Imagery Israel         | Survey 2 m (or Survey 2 m modern) | 2 m photos of Israel, if setup converted them                                                       |
| Imagery outside Israel | Sentinel-2 (or Sentinel-2 modern) | 10 m satellite imagery outside Israel, if setup converted it                                        |

A greyed-out imagery choice means its data was not converted (`tools/setup.sh --imagery`).

## 2. The jet in numbers

The F-35I row (docs/f35i.md §2, docs/real-aircraft.md) is checked by the game's validation test. **(U)** marks an
estimate.

| Item                                         | In the game                | Real F-35A / F-35I                          |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Empty weight                                 | 29,300 lb                  | 29,300 lb                                   |
| Internal fuel                                | 18,250 lb                  | 18,250 lb                                   |
| Thrust, military / full afterburner          | 28,000 / 43,000 lbf        | 28,000 / 43,000 lbf uninstalled (F135)      |
| 1 g stall speed, sea level                   | 122 kt                     | about 120–125 kt (U; approach about 150 kt) |
| g limits                                     | +9 / −3                    | +9 / −3 (U)                                 |
| Max roll rate                                | the F-16's flight controls | unpublished                                 |
| Max speed, sea level                         | 708 kt (Mach 1.07)         | about 700 kt (Mach 1.06)                    |
| Max speed, 40,000 ft                         | 905 kt (Mach 1.58)         | Mach 1.6                                    |
| Climb at 350 kt, sea level, full afterburner | about 32,700 ft/min        | unpublished                                 |
| Instantaneous turn, 420 kt at 10,000 ft      | 21.1°/s at 6.9 g           | unpublished                                 |
| Fuel flow, military / full afterburner       | 22,000 / 86,000 lb/h       | about 20,000–25,000 / 86,000 lb/h (U)       |
| Service ceiling                              | 50,000 ft                  | 50,000 ft                                   |

What that means in the cockpit:

- **Fuel.** The big internal tank lasts about 13 minutes at full afterburner, or about 50 minutes at military power
  at sea level: far longer than the 1998 jets.
- **Speeds the game's own training uses** (the same for every jet): rotate at **145 kt**, downwind **200 kt** at
  2,000 ft, base and final **165 kt**, gear down below **300 kt**. The real F-35A approaches at about **150 kt** at 13°
  angle of attack (U), so the training's 165 kt is a little fast.
- **No drag chute**: IAF F-35s have none.
- **Nose-wheel steering** from the rudder pedals, ±32° (the F-16's, U), wheelbase 17.6 ft.

## 3. Start-up, take-off and landing

### On the runway

A mission that starts at an airbase puts you on the runway with the gear down, full flaps, the brakes on and the
throttle at idle, the engine running.

### Take-off

1. Check the gear is down and the flaps are out (**F**).
2. Release the brakes (**B**).
3. Throttle to full afterburner: **8**. The afterburner lights after the engine reaches 100 %.
4. Keep the centreline with the rudder (**Numpad 0** / **Decimal**, or the pedals).
5. At **145 kt**, pull gently back until the flight path marker is above the horizon.
6. Gear up (**G**) and flaps up (**F**).

![Take-off roll](shots/f35i/takeoff_roll.png)

### Landing

1. **Downwind**: 2,000 ft above the ground, **200 kt**. Gear down (**G**), flaps out (**F**).
2. **Base**: throttle 60–80 % (keys **2** to **4**), slow to **165 kt**, aim for a 4° approach.
3. **Final**: **150–165 kt**, the flight path marker on the runway threshold.
4. Over the threshold: throttle to idle (**1**) and flare gently.
5. After touchdown: brakes (**B**).

With Real landing limits and Easy landing off, a touchdown faster than **4 m/s (about 790 ft/min)** or more than
**15° nose-up** is a crash.

![Final approach, gear down](shots/f35i/landing_final.png)

### Tower and autopilot

- **Ctrl+T** calls the tower near an Israeli base.
- **A** cycles the autopilot: off → level → NAV → off. **W** / **Shift+W** pick the next / previous waypoint.

## 4. Handling

- **Fly-by-wire**, like the F-16's: a normal pull does not stall or spin it. With the "F-16 / Lavi deep stall" switch
  on, the F-35I gets the F-16's deep stall too (zoom nose-high until the speed is gone; recover by rocking the stick in
  phase with the nose). The departure behaviour is the F-16's, a stand-in from the closest public data.
- **The stick on the keyboard** is all or nothing; a joystick is much smoother.
- **Flight-path hold.** With the stick centred the jet holds its flight path.
- **g-effects.** With No blackouts off, a hard pull greys and blacks out the screen.

## 5. The helmet display (the Real HUD)

The F-35's HUD is its Gen III helmet: it shows a green **virtual HUD** fixed to the airframe when you look forward
(40° × 30°, from Lockheed Martin's cockpit slides and the US Navy flight-test videos). With the Real HUD off the helmet shows the 1998 HUD symbology.

![The helmet's virtual HUD in NAV](shots/f35i/hud_nav.png)

- **Heading** tape at the top with a boxed heading.
- **Airspeed**: a boxed number on the left, no tape; under it ground speed `GS`, Mach `M`, `G`, angle of attack `α`
  and the maximum g.
- **Altitude**: a boxed number on the right (small last three digits); under it the vertical velocity and the radar
  altitude `R`.
- **Pitch ladder**: climb rungs solid, dive rungs dashed, the `-W-` waterline, the flight path marker, a bank scale
  at the bottom.
- **Lower left**: the master mode (`AA1`), the weapon (`2 AIM-A`), `ARM`. **Lower right**: the steerpoint (number,
  bearing / range) and the time.
- **Looking aside** (**Shift+arrows**, **F3**): the virtual HUD stays with the airframe and a reduced set rides your
  head: airspeed, altitude, heading, the steerpoint and the targets.
- The gun and air-to-ground graphics are the F-16's, as a reconstruction.

## 6. Radar (AN/APG-81)

With Weapon data = Real the radar detects at **148 km** (U: multiple-target track beyond 80 NM). The keys are every
jet's:

| Key                           | Action                                              |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **R**                         | Radar on: A-A, then toggles A-A ↔ A-G               |
| **Q**                         | Mode: A-A LRS → TWS → ACM; A-G GMT ↔ MAP            |
| **S**                         | Standby                                             |
| **.** / **,**                 | Range scale up / down                               |
| **Return** / **Shift+Return** | Next / previous contact (in LRS this locks it: STT) |
| Click a blip                  | Lock that contact                                   |
| **Backspace**                 | Drop the lock                                       |
| **\\** (hold)                 | Boresight                                           |

![The radar portal with a locked contact](shots/f35i/mfd_radar_stt.png)

## 7. The panoramic display

One wide touchscreen, as in the real jet (20 × 8 in), split into **four portals**: from the left the **radar**, the
**TSD** (tactical map), the **stores** page and the **RWR**. Click a portal's buttons as on any MFD. **T** brings up
the TSD, **D** the damage report, **V** / **PgUp** / **PgDn** slide the panel.

---

## 8. Self-protection

- **RWR**: the right-hand portal shows emitters and missiles around you; a new threat sounds a tone, a missile launch
  at you the launch warning.
- **Chaff (Insert)** and **flares (Delete)**: **60 chaff and 60 flares**. The decoy rules are every jet's: chaff 10 %
  (30 % above 4 g) against a radar missile, a flare 33 % (50 % above 4 g) against an IR missile, never with the
  afterburner lit.
- **No ECM** in the game: only the F-16, the Lavi and jets with the ECM pod can jam.

## 9. Weapons

**M** cycles the master mode NAV → A-A → A-G, and **N** returns to NAV. **]** selects the next air-to-air weapon and
**[** the next air-to-ground one. **Space** fires the selected weapon and **Tab** fires the gun. Nothing fires with the
gear handle down.

The F-35I has its own Arming screen and counts. Stations 1 and 9 are the wingtips, 2 and 8 the inner-wing heavy
stations, **3 / 7 and 4 / 6 the weapon bays** (outboard / inboard). Bay stores show only while the doors are open: a
release waits 0.5 s for them to open and they close 2 s later.

| Store (the game's stand-in for the real one)      | Stations         |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| AMRAAM (AIM-120)                                  | 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8 |
| AIM-9L (for the AIM-9X), Python 4                 | 1, 9             |
| MK-84L / MK-83L laser bombs (for GBU-31 / GBU-32) | 2, 3, 7, 8       |
| MK-84 / MK-83                                     | 2, 3, 7, 8       |
| GBU-15 (TV; for SPICE 1000)                       | 2, 8             |

Default load: 2 × AMRAAM in the inboard bays, 2 × MK-84L in the outboard bays.

### Gun (GAU-22/A)

The game's 20 mm gun stands in for the 25 mm GAU-22/A, with the real **180 rounds**.

- **A-A gun** (**]** to the gun) and **A-G gun** (**[** to the gun): the F-16's EEGS funnel and strafe pipper.

![The gun with a locked target](shots/f35i/hud_gun.png)

### IR missiles: AIM-9L, Python 4

| Missile  | Real data                                  |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| AIM-9L   | 86 kg, Mach 2.5, 35.4 km                   |
| Python 4 | 120 kg, Mach 3.5, 15 km, 60° off-boresight |

1. **]** to the missile: a growl, then a higher tone when the seeker **locks**.
2. **With a radar lock** the seeker is slaved to the locked target within its cone; **looking aside** (**Shift+arrows**,
   **F3**) the helmet cues it to the target you look at.
3. Beyond **half** its range the seeker sees only a jet **with its afterburner lit**.
4. Fire with **Space** when locked.

![AIM-9L with the seeker locked](shots/f35i/hud_srm.png)

### Radar missiles: AMRAAM

Real data: 157 kg, Mach 4, 70 km, active. Lock a target (**R**, **Return**), **]** to the AMRAAM: the helmet shows the
target designator (an X over a circle), the **steering circle** with its dot and the **DLZ** bracket with the range and
the closure. Steer the dot into the circle inside the DLZ and fire (**Space**): the bay opens, the missile drops.

![AMRAAM: the steering circle and the DLZ](shots/f35i/hud_mrm.png)

### Bombs

MK-84 / MK-83 and the laser bombs. The F-35I carries **no FLIR pod**, so in the game its laser bombs cannot be
designated: they fall like normal bombs (CCIP). **[** to the bomb, put the pipper on the target, hold **Space**.

![Bombing: the pipper on a target](shots/f35i/hud_bomb.png)

### TV weapon: GBU-15

**[** to it: the TV page shows its camera; slew with **Ctrl+arrows**, zoom with **Numpad +/−**, fire with **Space**;
it flies where the centre of the picture points.

### Jettison

**Shift+C**: the first press drops the fuel tanks, the second the bombs.
