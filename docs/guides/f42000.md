# Kurnass 2000 pilot guide (realistic settings)

This guide is for flying the Kurnass 2000 (the IAF's F-4E Phantom 2000 upgrade: the game's Reference text, "IAI
(McDonnell Douglas) F-4 Phantom 2000 upgrade", with the APG-76 radar, a wide-angle HUD and Elbit multifunction
displays; the model is the F-4E's two-seat Phantom) in this game with every realistic option switched on. The numbers
come from the game's own data and tests. Where the game differs from the real jet, the guide says so.

![The Kurnass 2000 cockpit on the runway](shots/f42000/cockpit_runway.png)

---

## 1. Settings

Set these on the Preferences screen before the flight. The original 1998 game is the default everywhere, so each of
these has to be switched on.

### Extras tab

| Row                    | Set to                            | What it does                                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Flight data            | Real aircraft                     | The F-4E (2 × J79-GE-17) numbers, which the Kurnass 2000 kept: weight, fuel, thrust, roll, g limits, fuel flow, ceiling, drag chute |
| Weapon data            | Real weapons                      | Real missile weights, speeds and ranges, the APG-76 radar range, 639 gun rounds at 6,000 rounds/min |
| HUD                    | Real                              | A wide-angle HUD; its layout is not public, so ours is an F-16-style reconstruction |
| HUD pitch ladder       | Conformal                         | The ladder lies on the real horizon (only matters with the original HUD)                            |
| Sky                    | Atmospheric                       | A physical sky with the sun, and haze that takes its colour                                         |
| Terrain close up       | Detailed                          | Ground detail below about 500 m                                                                     |
| Imagery Israel         | Survey 2 m (or Survey 2 m modern) | 2 m photos of Israel, if setup converted them                                                       |
| Imagery outside Israel | Sentinel-2 (or Sentinel-2 modern) | 10 m satellite imagery outside Israel, if setup converted it                                        |

A greyed-out imagery choice means its data was not converted (`tools/setup.sh --imagery`).

## 2. The jet in numbers

The Kurnass 2000 kept the F-4E's airframe and engines, so Flight data = Real gives it the F-4E row (see the F-4E
guide for the sources):

| Item                                   | In the game                  | Real F-4E (the same airframe)                                              |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Empty weight                           | 30,330 lb                    | about 30,330 lb (Jane's)                               |
| Internal fuel                          | 12,060 lb                    | 1,855 gal = 12,060 lb (block 41+)                      |
| Thrust, military / full afterburner    | 23,740 / 35,800 lbf          | 2 × 11,870 / 2 × 17,900 lbf (J79-GE-17)                |
| 1 g stall speed                        | 145 kt (the original's)      | about 150–165 kt at 40,000 lb (uncertain)              |
| g limits                               | +7.33 / −3                   | +7.33 at 37,500 lb or less / −3 (T.O. 1F-4E-1)         |
| Max roll rate                          | 150°/s (uncertain)           | about 120–180°/s at 350–450 kt (uncertain)             |
| Max speed, sea level                   | 780 kt (Mach 1.18)           | about 750 KIAS placard (Mach 1.13–1.19)                |
| Max speed, 40,000 ft                   | 1,239 kt (Mach 2.16)         | Mach 2.2–2.23                                          |
| Climb at 350 kt, sea level             | about 28,300 ft/min          | 41,300 ft/min                                          |
| Fuel flow, military / full afterburner | 20,000 / 71,000 lb/h         | about 20,000 / about 71,000 lb/h                       |
| Service ceiling                        | 58,750 ft                    | 58,750 ft at maximum power (100 ft/min)                |

What that means in the cockpit:

- **Fuel.** At full afterburner the internal fuel lasts about 10 minutes, at military power about 36 minutes. The
  3,700 lb tanks ("3700LB") go on stations 1, 5 and 9.
- **Drag chute (Shift+B)**: with Flight data = Real the 16 ft ring-slot chute adds real drag (deploy below 200 KIAS, the
  T.O. limit; there is no failure model).
- **Nose-wheel steering** comes from the rudder pedals, ±70° (below about 70 kt on the real jet), wheelbase 23.3 ft.
- **Two engines**: two sets of RPM, temperature and throttle needles on the panel.

## 3. Start-up, take-off and landing

### On the runway

A mission that starts at an airbase puts you on the runway with the gear down, full flaps, the brakes on and the
throttle at idle. The engines are already running at the lineup point. If you start elsewhere on the base, the first
throttle key starts them, and the RPM then climbs 15 % per second.

### Take-off

1. Check the gear is down and the flaps are out (**F**).
2. Release the brakes (**B**).
3. Throttle to full afterburner: **8**. The afterburner lights only after the engines reach 100 %, about 2 s from
   military power.
4. Keep the runway centreline with the rudder (**Numpad 0** / **Decimal**, or the pedals).
5. At **145 kt**, pull gently back until the flight path is above the horizon.
6. Gear up (**G**) and flaps up (**F**). Keep the gear speed below 300 kt.

### Landing

1. **Downwind**: 2,000 ft above the ground, **200 kt**. Gear down (**G**), flaps out (**F**).
2. **Base**: throttle 60–80 % (keys **2** to **4**), about 20° of bank, slow to **165 kt**, aim for a 4° approach.
3. **Final**: line up with the runway on a 4° glide slope: put the flight path marker on the cross of the ILS lines on the HUD (the localizer and a 4° glide slope).
4. Over the threshold: throttle to idle (**1**) and flare gently.
5. After touchdown: brakes (**B**); then the drag chute (**Shift+B**) below 200 kt.

With Real landing limits and Easy landing off, a touchdown faster than **4 m/s (about 790 ft/min)** or more than
**15° nose-up** is a crash. These speeds come from the game's own training lessons, which are the same for every jet.

![Final approach, gear down](shots/f42000/landing_final.png)

### Tower and autopilot

- **Ctrl+T** calls the tower near an Israeli base.
- **A** cycles the autopilot: off → level → NAV (follows the waypoints) → off. **W** / **Shift+W** pick the next /
  previous waypoint.

## 4. Handling

- **The stick on the keyboard** is all or nothing: a tap gives full deflection at once, as in the original. A joystick
  is much smoother.
- **No fly-by-wire limiter.** Nothing stops you from pulling into a stall: at low speed a hard pull stalls the wings,
  and with Realistic spins on you can spin. Recover with the stick forward and the rudder against the spin, then pull
  out of the dive.
- **Flight-path hold.** With the stick centred the jet holds its flight path.
- **g-effects.** With No blackouts off, a hard pull greys and blacks out the screen.

## 5. The Real HUD

The real Kurnass 2000 has an El-Op (Kaiser licence) wide-angle HUD said to show speed, altitude, the direction of
flight and the wind, but **no symbology layout is public**. The Real switch therefore draws the **F-16's HUD** (an
F-16-style reconstruction, without a wind readout): everything in the F-16 guide's Real HUD section applies, including
the radar altitude box (`R`), the bank scale, the zenith star and nadir earth, **SHOOT** in the centre window, EEGS,
the ASEC / DLZ and CCIP.

![The Real HUD in NAV (an F-16-style reconstruction)](shots/f42000/hud_nav.png)

**Off-boresight**: the Kurnass 2000's cockpit has the **DASH** helmet sight: in free look (**Shift+arrows**) or padlock
(**F3**) the IR seeker can take the target you look at, within the missile's cone.

## 6. Radar (AN/APG-76)

With Weapon data = Real the radar detects at **44 km** (uncertain: 22–25 NM frontal, from one source). The keys are
the same as in every jet:

| Key                           | Action                                              |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **R**                         | Radar on: A-A, then toggles A-A ↔ A-G               |
| **Q**                         | Mode: A-A LRS → TWS → ACM; A-G GMT ↔ MAP            |
| **S**                         | Standby                                             |
| **.** / **,**                 | Range scale up / down                               |
| **Return** / **Shift+Return** | Next / previous contact (in LRS this locks it: STT) |
| Click a blip                  | Lock that contact                                   |
| **Backspace**                 | Drop the lock                                       |
| **\\** (hold)                | Boresight: a ±12° lock cone straight ahead          |

The radar does not see targets lower than 30 m above the ground or behind terrain. Locking a target tells it you
locked it (and its RWR sounds).

![The radar page with a locked contact](shots/f42000/mfd_radar_stt.png)

## 7. MFDs

The Kurnass 2000 has **two displays**: the left one starts on the **radar**, the right one on the **RWR** (there is no
separate RWR dial). The attitude ball is an MFD page (MENU → ADI). **T** brings up the TSD (tactical map), **D** the
damage report, **V** / **PgUp** / **PgDn** slide the panel. Picking a weapon brings up its page: the stores page for
bombs and rockets, the FLIR, TV or HARM page for those weapons.

---

## 8. Self-protection

- **RWR**: the right display shows emitters and missiles around you; a new threat sounds a tone, a missile launch at
  you the launch warning.
- **Chaff (Insert)** and **flares (Delete)**: the Kurnass 2000 carries **120 chaff and 60 flares**. Each press releases
  one. At release, every missile of the decoy's kind launched at you rolls once: chaff fools a radar missile with 10 %
  (30 % above 4 g), a flare an IR missile with 33 % (50 % above 4 g), and **never with the afterburner lit**.
- **ECM (J)**: only with the **ECM pod** loaded on station 5 or 6. Switching it on jams each radar-guided missile already
  flying at you with a 60 % chance. It has no effect on SAMs.

## 9. Weapons

**M** cycles the master mode NAV → A-A → A-G, and **N** returns to NAV. **]** selects the next air-to-air weapon and
**[** the next air-to-ground one. **Space** fires the selected weapon and **Tab** fires the gun. Nothing fires with the
gear handle down.

What the Kurnass 2000 can load on the Arming screen (stations 1 and 9 are the outer wing pylons, 2 and 8 the inner
ones, 3, 4, 6, 7 the fuselage, 5 the centreline):

| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |

| AIM-7M Sparrow                           | 3, 4, 6, 7                                  |
| AGM-88 HARM                              | 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8                            |
| AGM-65 Maverick (×3)                     | 2, 8                                        |
| AGM-62, GBU-15, Popeye (TV)              | 1, 2, 8, 9                                  |
| MK-82L laser bomb                        | 1, 4, 6, 9, and ×3 on 2, 8                  |

### Gun (M61A1)

With Real data: **639 rounds**, 6,000 rounds/min, 1,030 m/s: about 6 s of firing. The Real HUD's **EEGS** (F-16
reconstruction): the funnel without a lock; with a radar lock the T-symbol, the 4 mr pipper and the range circle.

### IR missiles: AIM-9L / AIM-9M, Python 3

| Missile         | Real data                                  |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| AIM-9L / AIM-9M | 86 kg, Mach 2.5, 35.4 km                   |
| Python 3        | 120 kg, Mach 3.5, 15 km, 30° off-boresight |

1. **]** until `SRM`: a growl, then a higher tone when the seeker **locks**.
2. **With a radar lock** the seeker is slaved to the locked target within its cone off the nose.
3. Beyond **half** its range the seeker sees only a jet **with its afterburner lit**.
4. Fire with **Space** when locked; locked and in range the Real HUD shows **SHOOT**.

![Python 3 with the seeker locked](shots/f42000/hud_srm.png)

### Radar missile: AIM-7M Sparrow

Real data: 230 kg, Mach 4, 70 km, **semi-active**: needs your lock until it hits.

1. Radar on (**R**), lock a target.
2. **]** until `MRM`: the **ASEC** circle with its steering dot and the **DLZ** on the right.
3. Inside the DLZ steer the dot into the circle: **SHOOT** comes up. Fire (**Space**) and **keep the lock**.

![AIM-7M: ASEC and the DLZ](shots/f42000/hud_mrm.png)

### Bombs and rockets: CCIP

1. **[** until `CCIP`. The pipper is where the bomb will hit, joined to the flight path marker by the fall line; a
   hill between you and the impact takes the pipper.
2. Put the pipper on the target and hold **Space**: the ripple releases one store per interval while you hold it.
3. **Delayed release**: if the impact is below the HUD's bottom edge, hold **Space** with the target under the pipper
   at the edge; the bombs go when the time-to-go reaches 0.9 s (positive g, less than 90° of bank).
4. The ripple quantity and spacing are set on the stores page (default: 2 bombs, 10 m apart).

The game's training warns against GP bombs below 2,000 ft above the ground. There is no fuse or arming time: a bomb
dropped from any height bursts at the ground.

![CCIP with the pipper on a target](shots/f42000/hud_ccip.png)

### Laser bombs: MK-82L, MK-83L, MK-84L

These need the **FLIR pod** on station 4.

1. **[** to the laser bomb: the FLIR page comes up.
2. Slew the FLIR camera with **Ctrl+arrows**; release the keys to lock it on the point in the middle. **Numpad +/−**
   zoom, a page button switches WIDE / SPOT.
3. **L** turns the laser on (`LASER ON`): the bomb flies to the camera's centre point if it is within 60° of the bomb's
   line and on the ground. With the laser off it falls like a normal bomb (CCIP).
4. Release with **Space** as for CCIP.

![The FLIR page, laser on](shots/f42000/mfd_flir.png)

### TV weapons: AGM-65 Maverick, AGM-62, GBU-15, Popeye

1. **[** to the TV weapon: the TV page shows the weapon's camera, and the HUD a diamond at its aim point.
2. Slew with **Ctrl+arrows**, zoom with **Numpad +/−**; **Z** makes the TV picture full screen.
3. Fire with **Space**. The **Maverick** (Real: 209 kg, over 27 km) homes by itself; the **AGM-62, GBU-15 and
   Popeye** are flown by the camera (TRA, then TER near the aim).

![The TV page (Maverick)](shots/f42000/mfd_tv.png)

### HARM: AGM-88

Real data: 360 kg, Mach 2, 150 km. **[** to the AGM-88: the HARM page lists the radar sites inside a ±15° cone ahead,
out to 20 NM; **Ctrl+Return** steps to the next target. Fire with **Space** when the target's diamond is near the HUD
centre.

![The HARM page](shots/f42000/mfd_harm.png)

### Jettison

**Shift+C**: the first press drops the fuel tanks, the second the bombs.
