# F-15I Ra'am pilot guide (realistic settings)

This guide covers our **F-15I Ra'am** ("Thunder"), the IAF's (Israeli Air Force) two-seat strike Eagle with conformal
fuel tanks, flown with the realistic options on. The Ra'am is our addition to the game (docs/f15i.md): the 1998 game
has no Ra'am. It is today's upgraded F-15I, and where the F-15I has no published figure the game uses the F-15E's
(the jet it is built from), labelled as such. It uses the F-15's cockpit and HUD (head-up display), so this guide
covers what differs from the F-15C Baz and points to the [F-15 guide](f15.md) for the rest: the HUD modes, the DASH
helmet, the missiles, the gun and the bombs. Section 7 lists the keys that differ.

**To fly it**: Preferences → Extras → **F-15 version** → **Ra'am (F-15I)**. Every F-15 becomes an F-15I: yours, your
wingmen's, the enemy's and the parked ones, in every mission. With the switch on, the Reference screen's **PILOT
GUIDE** for the F-15 shows this guide; set the switch back to Baz (F-15C) to read the F-15 guide there.

## 1. What's different in this jet

| Area | F-15C Baz (Real flight data) | F-15I Ra'am |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Airframe | One seat | Two seats under one canopy, conformal fuel tanks (CFTs) on the intakes' sides |
| Engines | 2 × F100-PW-220: 28,740 / 46,900 lbf | 2 × F100-PW-229: 35,600 / 58,320 lbf |
| Fuel | 13,455 lb internal | 22,665 lb: 12,915 lb internal and 2 × 4,875 lb in the CFTs (F-15E) |
| Weight | 28,476 lb empty | 37,000 lb with the CFTs and crew (F-15E), 81,000 lb maximum |
| Radar | AN/APG-63: 135 km (Weapon data = Real) | AN/APG-70I: 104 km (56 NM) |
| Gun | M61A1, 940 rounds (Weapon data = Real) | M61A1, 500 rounds (F-15E) |
| IR missiles | AIM-9L / M, Python 3 / 4 | Also the **Python 5**: 100° off the nose, lock-on after launch |
| Bombs | Free-fall, laser and TV weapons | Also the **GBU-31V3**: a GPS-guided 2,000 lb BLU-109 penetrator (JDAM) |
| Flight data | Original or Real | Always the Ra'am's real numbers, whatever Flight data says |

![The F-15I Ra'am: the two-seat canopy, the conformal fuel tanks, Python 5s and GBU-31(V)3s](shots/f15i/external.png)

The game has no second cockpit: you fly from the front seat, and the WSO's (weapon systems officer's) work is yours.

**Voice and HUD warnings**: as on the F-15 ([F-15 guide §1](f15.md#1-whats-different-in-this-jet)).

## 2. Settings

Set the F-15 guide's rows ([F-15 guide §2](f15.md#2-settings)), and these:

| Tab | Row | Set to | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Extras | F-15 version | Ra'am (F-15I) | Every F-15 is an F-15I: its flight data (section 3), radar (section 4), gun and weapons (section 5), the model, the Arming screen's art and weights (section 6) |
| Extras | Weapon data | Real weapons | The Python 5 on the Arming screen, the radar's 104 km, the gun's 500 rounds |

- **Flight data** has no effect on the Ra'am: the 1998 game has no Ra'am data, so it always flies its real numbers.
- **Weapons** (Original / Modern) has no effect on the Ra'am's own weapons: it always lists them.
- **Models**: the Ra'am is drawn with the F-15I model setup makes under both settings (Original and Detailed).

## 3. Flying the F-15I

These are the game's values, checked by its validation test (the F-15I row) against the published figures. The F-15C
column is the F-15 with Flight data = Real ([F-15 guide §4](f15.md#4-flying-the-f-15-with-real-flight-data)).

| Item | F-15I in the game | F-15C in the game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Empty weight | 37,000 lb (F-15E, with the CFTs and crew) | 28,476 lb |
| Internal fuel | 22,665 lb | 13,455 lb |
| Weight with full fuel, no stores | 59,665 lb | 41,931 lb |
| Thrust, military / full afterburner | 35,600 / 58,320 lbf | 28,740 / 46,900 lbf |
| 1 g stall speed, sea level, full fuel | 155 kt (F-15E, engine idle, gear and flaps up) | 130 kt |
| Maximum roll rate | 180°/s | 180°/s |
| Maximum speed, sea level / 40,000 ft | 765 kt (Mach 1.16) / 1,133 kt (Mach 1.98) | 805 kt / 1,238 kt |
| Climb at 350 kt, sea level, full afterburner | about 37,300 ft/min | about 42,000 ft/min |
| Instantaneous turn, 420 kt at 10,000 ft | 16.1°/s at 6.4 g | 22.4°/s at 8.7 g |
| g limits | +8.1 / −2.0 (F-15E with the CFTs) | +9 / −3 |
| Fuel flow, military / full afterburner | 27,000 / 113,100 lb/h | 21,000 / 98,000 lb/h |
| Service ceiling | 60,000 ft (F-15E) | 65,000 ft |

The Ra'am's numbers and their sources: docs/f15i.md §1. The top speed at 40,000 ft is fitted to the F-15E's
Mach 2.0 with the CFTs, a secondary figure. The stall speed, the CFTs' drag and the g limits are the F-15E flight
manual's.

What this means in the cockpit:

- **Fuel**: about 12 minutes at full afterburner and about 50 minutes at military power at sea level (the F-15C: 8
  and 38). The Ra'am is built for the long strike.
- **Weight**: at full fuel the Ra'am is about 42 % heavier for 24 % more thrust. It climbs and accelerates more slowly
  and turns less hard than the Baz. As the fuel burns down it comes closer.
- **Turns**: the g limit is 8.1, not the Baz's 9 (the F-15E's with the CFTs). At full fuel it reaches 8.1 g only
  from about 440 kt (inferred: √8.1 × the stall speed); fight faster than in the Baz.
- **Top speed**: lower than the Baz's: the CFTs add drag.
- **Engines**: with Physics "Engine spool-up per real engine" on, the F100-PW-229s go from idle to full afterburner in 3.9 s (the
  F-15E's "less than four seconds"), the Baz's -220s in 5.5 s.
- **Everything else** is the F-15's: the mechanical flight controls, the flight-path hold, ground effect, the wind, the brakes, unbalanced stores and Bomb release Real
  ([F-15 guide §4](f15.md#4-flying-the-f-15-with-real-flight-data)).

### Take-off and landing numbers

| Item | F-15I |
| --- | --- |
| Rotate | about 170 kt at full fuel (inferred: the F-15's 145 kt scaled by the weight) |
| Gear up below | 300 kt |
| Downwind / final | 200 kt at 2,000 ft above the ground / about 170 kt with 4,000 lb of fuel left (inferred) |
| 1 g stall, sea level, full fuel | 155 kt |
| Touchdown limits (Real landing limits, Easy landing off) | sink below 4 m/s (790 ft/min), pitch below 15° |
| Nose-wheel steering | rudder pedals, ±45° |

## 4. Radar

The Ra'am's AN/APG-70I has the APG-70's air-to-air modes; its ground maps have a third of the USAF set's
resolution[^wp-e]. It sees a fighter-sized target at 56 NM (104 km)[^wp-e]. The modes, keys, the RWR, chaff, flares
and ECM are the F-15's ([F-15 guide §5](f15.md#5-radar-displays-and-self-protection)).

| Item | F-15I | F-15C |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Detection, Weapon data = Real (LRS and STT) | 104 km (56 NM) | 135 km (73 NM) |
| The AI's sensor, an AI F-15 | 30.8 NM | 40 NM |
| Detection, Weapon data = Original | the F-15's | the F-15's |

With Real weapon data the Ra'am's radar sees less far than the Baz's: the two figures come from different sources
(docs/f15i.md §2).

## 5. Weapons

The Arming screen lists the F-15's weapons and the Ra'am's own:

| Weapon | Stations | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Python 5** | two on each wing pylon's rails (1 and 9) | Weapon data = Real only; IAF photos show it on F-15Is[^avi24] |
| **GBU-31V3** | the wing pylons (2, 8) and the CFT stations (3, 4, 6, 7): six | the BLU-109 JDAM, reported six per jet in 2024[^armyrec] |
| AMRAAM, AIM-7, AIM-9L / M, Python 3 / 4 | as the F-15 | |
| Mk 82 / 83 / 84, laser and TV bombs, tanks | as the F-15 | |

**Python 5**: it flies, locks and cues as on the F-16I ([F-16I guide](f16i.md#aim-9x-and-python-5)): the DASH
cues it up to 100° off the nose, and fired without a lock it locks on after launch. The keys are the F-15's SRM keys
([F-15 guide §7](f15.md#7-srm-aim-9l--aim-9m-python-3-python-4)).

**GBU-31V3**: a JDAM, as the F-15 guide's GBU-31 ([F-15 guide](f15.md#gbu-31-jdam-weapons--modern)): PRE targets
from the briefing TSD, VIS targets from the radar's MAP, the LAR staple and the time to max range on the HUD. Its
BLU-109 body has less explosive than the Mk 84 (250 against 429 kg), and the game's blast scales with it.

![A GBU-31(V)3 in PRE 18 km from its target: the LAR staple and the time to max range](shots/f15i/hud_jdam.png)

1. **TD box**: the bomb's target.
2. **Azimuth steering line**: put the velocity vector on it.
3. **Velocity vector**.
4. **LAR staple**: the ranges the bomb can reach, Rmin at its bottom, Rmax at its top.
5. **Range caret** under the scale's top (10 NM): your ground distance to the target.
6. **TMR 0:45**: the time to max range; inside the LAR it reads `IN RNG`.
7. **PRE**: the mode, under the G.

**Example**: the picture. Two GBU-31V3s on the wing pylons; the PRE target lies 18 km ahead, 4° right; you dive
gently at 486 kt from 10,000 ft. The caret sits above the staple and `TMR 0:45` counts down. Hold the velocity vector
on the steering line; when `IN RNG` shows, press **Space**: the bomb leaves at once and glides onto the target.

**Gun**: the F-15's M61A1 with 500 rounds (Weapon data = Real), the F-15E's count: about 5 s of firing.

Not in the game yet: the GBU-28 and the SPICE 1000, which sources put on the F-15I (docs/f15i.md §3).

## 6. The Arming screen

The Ra'am has its own Arming art: its front view with the conformal tanks, the F-15's nine station boxes, base
weight 59,734 lb and maximum take-off weight 81,000 lb (F-15E). Setup draws the art from the F-15I model; until setup
has run again the screen shows the F-15's art with the Ra'am's weights.

## 7. Keys

The keys are the F-15's ([F-15 guide §14](f15.md#14-key-card)). The Python 5 uses the SRM keys, the GBU-31V3 the
GPS bomb keys: **[** to select it, **K** PRE ↔ VIS, **Shift+K** the next PRE target, **Space** to release.
